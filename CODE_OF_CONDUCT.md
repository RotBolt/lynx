# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep discussions respectful,
constructive, and focused on improving Lynx for developers and AI agents.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberate exposure
of private application data are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when this standard is violated.

Report concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer
contact channel rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
